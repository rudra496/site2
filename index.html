Featured Research · August 2026 Journal of Ethnopharmacology (Elsevier · Q1) · Vol. 373, Art. 122316

Ethnoveterinary Herbal Practices Are Associated with Reduced Within-Farm Antibiotic Use and Improved Farm Performance in Commercial Poultry in Bangladesh: A Multi-District Cross-Sectional Study

Our nationwide investigation evaluated 795 commercial poultry farms across 35 districts representing all 8 administrative divisions of Bangladesh. The empirical baseline reveals a 43.6% within-group reduction in antibiotic use, lower Feed Conversion Ratios (FCR), reduced flock mortality, and critical food safety insights under the One Health framework.
