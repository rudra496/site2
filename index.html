Peer-Reviewed Article · Elsevier August 24, 2026 · Vol. 373, Art. 122316

Ethnoveterinary Herbal Practices Are Associated with Reduced Within-Farm Antibiotic Use and Improved Farm Performance in Commercial Poultry in Bangladesh: A Multi-District Cross-Sectional Study

Jubayer Mahmud Nirjus (PSTU), Mousumi Saha (GAU), Rudra Sarker (SUST) · Journal of Ethnopharmacology (Impact Factor: ~5.4, CiteScore: ~9.2)

A nationwide empirical investigation evaluating 795 commercial poultry farms across 35 districts representing all 8 administrative divisions of Bangladesh. The baseline demonstrates an observational 43.6% within-group reduction in self-reported antibiotic use, lower Feed Conversion Ratios (FCR 1.970 vs 2.144, p < 0.001), lower flock mortality (3.42% vs 5.97%, p < 0.001), and critical food safety awareness gaps under the One Health framework.
